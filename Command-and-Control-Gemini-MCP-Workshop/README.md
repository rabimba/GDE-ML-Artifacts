## 🚀 Quick Start: Presenting Live Tomorrow

### Option 1: Present Directly from Browser (Interactive Mode + Dual-Screen Notes)
```bash
cd Command-and-Control-Gemini-MCP-Workshop
./present.sh
```
- Press **`P`** inside the deck to pop out the **Synchronized Speaker Notes Console** onto your laptop screen while keeping `index.html` full-screen (`F`) on the projector!
- Or press **`S`** to toggle the built-in Speaker Notes Teleprompter drawer at the bottom of the window.

### Option 2: Present from Google Slides or PowerPoint
1. Upload `Command_and_Control_Gemini_MCP_Workshop.pptx` to Google Drive and open with **Google Slides** (or open directly in PowerPoint / Keynote).
2. Click **Slideshow → Presenter view** to see your embedded slide-by-slide speaker script and timing cues.
